Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Thank you for playing PathWord. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

PathWord does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game is designed to work offline and does not require account creation or login.

Game Data

Your game progress, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up using your own device backup services.

Permissions

PathWord only requests permissions that are necessary for the app to function properly. The app does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location unless a future feature explicitly requires it.

Third-Party Services

Currently, PathWord does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

PathWord does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

Data Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, your privacy remains protected while using the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
