Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

TaskFlow ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TaskFlow mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or contact details. All tasks and data you create are stored locally on your device.

2. Local Data Storage

TaskFlow stores your tasks, reminders, and preferences locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms. This data is not shared with any external servers.

3. Notifications

The app may send local notifications to remind you about your tasks. These notifications are generated on your device and do not involve any external data transfer.

4. Permissions

TaskFlow may request the following permissions:

• Notification access – to send task reminders
• (Optional) Location – for context-based features (home/office mode)

5. Third-Party Services

TaskFlow does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

6. Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

TaskFlow does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
